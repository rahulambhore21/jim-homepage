What is included in your Web Services offerings?

We build fast, SEO-ready business websites, multi-course EdTech academies with certificate verification, and conversion-tuned D2C storefronts powered by Next.js, React, and ₹ INR checkout integrations.

How do Custom Software and Business Growth Software differ?

Custom Software solves bespoke operational workflows — like booking platforms, customer portals, and vehicle or product selectors — while Business Growth Software focuses on CRM pipelines, automated lead nurturing, dynamic pricing engines, and revenue dashboards.

What can WebMisTech AI Agents automate for our business?

Our autonomous AI Agents handle 24/7 customer inquiries on your website and WhatsApp, qualify sales prospects, book calendar or service slots, and answer technical product questions using your private company knowledge base.

What engagement models and ₹ INR pricing tiers do you offer?

We offer AI Agent pilots from ₹99,000, Growth Software & MVP sprints starting at ₹1,50,000, complete custom platform builds tailored to scope, and ongoing monthly engineering retainers billed transparently in ₹ INR with GST invoicing.

Do we retain 100% ownership of source code and software IP?

Yes. Every repository, database schema, Figma UI system, and AI prompt workflow is committed directly to your organization's GitHub/GitLab and cloud accounts under full IP assignment.
